# Shoshone Shredder: where things stand (Nov 1, 2026; one page added Nov 3; bike rules Nov 5; progression loop Nov 6; star targets Nov 7)

Read this first in a new session. It covers what's built, what we decided and why, what the boys did, the
open design threads, and how to check things. The design spec (Draft 2) is in `docs/spec-draft-2.md`.

## Nov 7-12: first area redesign (two places instead of one test park)

**Building another area? Read `docs/building-an-area.md` first.** It lists the lessons from this one.

Spec and plan are in `docs/superpowers/`; every ruling, retune and deferred item is in
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-07-first-area-redesign/progress.md`; the Task 12 verification (ladder, margins, checks) is in
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-07-first-area-redesign/task-12-report.md`.
- **Two places.** Shredder Hills (`shredderHills`, `src/world/places/hill.ts`) is the trail hill: Goat Ladder, Hairpin
  Hollow and Boulder Dash. Mesa Jump Park (`mesaJumpPark`, `src/world/places/park.ts`) holds the features: Table Rows,
  Whoop-de-Doo, Pump House, Cone Zone, the Plank. `src/world/places/index.ts` lists stations and builds a place by id;
  the map and hub take you to either. Progress sums stars over both areas.
- **The trail tool** (`src/world/trail.ts`). A trail is a `TrailPath` (straights and arcs) plus its features in trail
  coordinates (`u` along, `off` to the right): `placeFeatures` builds ledges, rocks, gravel, jumps, slabs and logs,
  `trailRibbon` the dirt, `obstacleSpans` the stretches with something you could ride round. Trail modules are in
  `src/world/trails/`. Rock shapes seed from trail coordinates, so moving a trail doesn't change how it rides.
  `src/world/terrain.ts` shapes the hillside round the trails (benches level with the trail, never poking above it).
- **Trail edges.** Tall grass beside the trails (`T.tallGrassDrag = 1.8`, 3x plain grass) slows a rider who leaves the
  dirt; plant clumps (firm bumpers) stand beside every obstacle span and round the outside of every switchback.
  `tests/edges.test.ts` now checks a lateral reach per obstacle span on every hill trail and round each switchback's
  outside (grass or something solid within 1.4 m), and `checks/ladder-robots.ts` has an edge rider (`e` rows).
- **Scenery and boundary.** Both places have permanent foothills scenery (the baked mountain backdrop, pines,
  boulders, a hawk). Rebake with `npx vite-node scripts/bake-backdrop.ts` after changing the pictures
  (`public/backdrop/README.md`). Each place ends at its plan extent plus about 30 m, where an invisible wall-tagged
  collider ring (3.5 m high) stops the bike; inside it the visual ground matches the collision ground and every pine and
  boulder is solid. The rule a kid can say back: "the place ends where the hills start."
- **Star-time changes (2 star only)**: Goat Ladder down 10.6 to 9.8 s, Hairpin up 78 to 74.5 s, Hairpin down 44 to 44.9 s,
  Hairpin both 130 to 126.4 s. The Task 12 ladder run needed no further retune. Fragile margins (bare hardtail vs 2 star):
  Goat down 0.07 s, Hairpin down 0.27 s, Pump House 0.18 s.
- **Climb zones ride one way (Nov 13).** A `TrailZone` can carry `up` (which way along the trail climbs), and the game
  and robots ask `inZoneHeading` (src/world/trail.ts), so a climb is only a climb ridden up. Before this, Hairpin
  Hollow's legs were climb zones both ways and the descent pedaled on climbing legs (about 5–10× less pickup). Both
  Hairpin legs and the Goat Ladder climb carry `up: 1`. The descent got about 10 s faster; retimed Hairpin down
  90 / 34.25 / 31.5 (was 44.9 / 42) and Hairpin both 240 / 117 / 104 (was 126.4 / 112). Hairpin down's 2 star is
  very tight on purpose: hardtail with all parts 34.22 s, the off-trail edge rider (enduro with parts) 34.28 s,
  bare hardtail 35.90 s. Hairpin up unchanged; the four-way rule holds on Goat up, Goat both, Hairpin down, Hairpin both.
- **Known exceptions.** Cone Zone: the bare hardtail still gets 2 star (bikes don't separate there). Goat Ladder down: an
  edge-rider enduro with parts rides the trail's own edge line to 9.33 s, under the 9.8 s 2 star (accepted: it stays on
  the dirt and is slower than the centre line).
- **For the user to know / judge:**
  - **The boundary wall** (Task 11 fix) is invisible. It may feel abrupt; a fence look or a soft push-back is the
    replacement if it does. A very high jump could clear 3.5 m.
  - **The park-to-hill return path is long, about 130 m.** Is it a chore?
  - **The Whoop-de-Doo run-out is straight into the Pump House, with no berm** (a berm only has a job where a line turns).
  - **The Plank sits on a hard pad** (a gravel "ground" slab) so the hop-chain guard can see flat ground; hopping along
    it still compounds, so a dirtjumper tapping Jump scores 1764 (3 star) there while the hardtail stays at 2 star.
  - Hairpin's top has an 80 m dirt spur to the Shoulder; full-stick riders can overshoot through its gap.
- **What to playtest with the boys.** Can they find the hill and the park from the map? Do the grass edges feel like
  "slow, not a wall"? Does the invisible wall surprise them? Is Hairpin Hollow's spur and the walk back obvious? Is Goat
  Ladder down's 2 star reachable on the bare hardtail only barely (it should be)? Does the Plank crowd out "manual, hop the
  gap"? Does anyone get stuck by a ramp or a rock?
- **Please measure on the iPad.** Open `http://192.168.1.223:5173/play.html?debug` and write down fps, draw calls, triangles
  and resolution at the Trailhead (the hill; heaviest in the budget, about 534k triangles / 66 draws in the desktop
  budget check) and at the Park lot, then playtest with the boys.
- **Final review fixes (Nov 12).** A star in one place now announces what it opened in the other ("Cone Zone at Mesa Jump
  Park"); zone tests are cheap (`TrailPath.probe`, one shared lookup per trail per step); the land past the boundary
  starts rising at the wall (`smooth(0, 80, out)`); the hill builds about 5x faster (`heightAt` bisects); the ladder's
  edge rider now says whether its best run left the ribbon. Details in
  `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-07-first-area-redesign/final-fix-report.md`.
- **Decisions for you.**
  - **M8, decided Nov 12: keep it.** The edge rider (enduro with parts) beats 2 star on Goat Ladder down at 9.33 s against
    9.8 s, on the trail ribbon. That breaks the plan's constraint that the edge rider never beats a trail challenge's
    2 star. You decided to keep it, with no retune: only 42 of 528 edge runs finish, so it comes down to skill or luck.
    (Caveat from the new off-ribbon check: that best 9.33 s run's bike centre goes 0.33 m past the ribbon's edge at one
    point, so the check now reads "BREAKS: e·d reach 2★ off the trail" for it. Brief and shallow, so still a keep.)
    (Option left open if it ever bothers you: tighten goatDown's 2 star.)
  - **Margins under 0.3 s.** Three 2-star margins are thin: goatDown 0.07 s, pumpHouse 0.18 s, hairpinDown 0.27 s. They
    are listed, not retuned. Say if you want any of them widened or tightened after the playtest.
  - **The boundary.** It now stands at the foot of a slope, so the invisible wall has a visible rise ahead of it (a gentle
    one in the headless screenshots: easy to miss from 14 m out). Does that feel right on the iPad, or should it
    be steeper, or get a split-rail fence?

## Nov 7: Park challenges score combos

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-combo-scoring-design.md`; plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-07-combo-scoring.md`;
rulings in `docs/superpowers/ledgers/2026-11-07-combo-scoring.md`.
- **One scoring rule everywhere.** A challenge's trick points are its banked combo totals (points × tricks, `comboTotal`)
  plus the combo still open when it ends. `trickTick(ev.banked, s.combo)` in `challenges.ts` builds the `Tick` fields
  (`bank`, `open`) for the ride and for every robot, so they can't drift. Starting a challenge clears the combo.
- **3★ needs chaining on the multi-jump lines.** A manual from each landing to the next takeoff keeps one combo going
  (`chainManual` robot styles in `checks/ladder-robots.ts`). Table Row Big and XL have one table, so their 3★ is big
  airs. The hardtail chains too (it's a skill, not a part); the ladder compares each loadout's best. New targets in
  the ladder table (rows **).
- **Points come from lines over features** (the whole game, free ride too; design
  `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-lines-over-features-design.md`). A combo is one line: it starts on a feature (a lip
  launch, or the `ramp` surface of the plank and kickers), lasts through air off a feature or a manual, and banks a
  second after either. On the flat with no line, tricks score nothing (`ev.off`; the ride says "Just for fun!" now and
  then and leaves them out of the combo line). Air that isn't off a feature lets the bank clock run. `s.onLine` and
  `air.feature` in `src/lab/bike.ts`. The final review found chained flat hops scoring 50·n² (430,000 on Table Row Big);
  the robots keep `hop chain guard` styles (taps Jump on every flat, with and without manuals between jumps) in TABLES
  and PLANK; under the model their best stays below the chaining lines. A hop scores by the air it starts
  (`scoreAir`: `air.feature`), a manual while a line is open (`scoreManual`). A trick that scores nothing plays no
  chime (its count is 0; the chime once read note -1, threw, and froze the ride).
  On the Plank, hopping along it compounds (each hop is off the plank, a feature): playtest whether it crowds out
  "manual, hop the gap".
- Text: the trick line leads with where tricks score ("Off the jumps:" or "On the plank:") and adds `Hop 50`; the timed banner counts down (`★★★ in 12 s`); shown points round down;
  "New star!" comes before 🔓; the unlock check reads the save's profile.
- Tests: 310 passing. `hub.mjs` 166, `game.mjs` 68. Park sweep: the rule holds on all six re-tuned challenges.

## Nov 7: a wipeout restarts a Park challenge; "You left the spot" on Whoop-de-Doo

- **A crash on a Park challenge starts it again from the beginning** (the boys asked): `tick` returns
  `{ state: 'restart' }`, the ride lets the wipeout play for 1.2 s (`RESTART_AFTER`), then counts down into the same
  challenge from its ring. No result card, no cogs. Trail and Downhill runs still get up where they fell (the clock
  runs). Robots count a Park crash as a failed ride: best scores unchanged, the ladder holds on the same 7 of 8 Park.
- **Why Whoop-de-Doo said "You left the spot" on the trail:** under crash-costs-time a crashed jump didn't count and
  the lines are one way, so after one crash the rider rode on to the zone's end, 0.7 m past the last landing with 8 m
  of dirt ahead. The restart removes the cause. The Rhythm zone also now runs to `RHYTHM.x0 + 94`, past the end of the
  dirt, and a test in `tests/spots.test.ts` holds every jump challenge's zone to 5 m of run-out after its last landing
  (a rolled double with no air can still leave you short; the card then says "4 of 5 jumps cleared").
- Tests: 300 passing. `game.mjs` 67 (the restart lines are new), `hub.mjs` 165.

## Nov 7: star targets and trick points

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-star-targets-design.md`; plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-07-star-targets.md`.
The boys didn't know what a star needs, which tricks pay most, or that a combo multiplies. Only words on screen changed:
no points, targets or rules.

- **Ring card:** under each open challenge's tip, what each star needs (`★ 150 · ★★ 1500 · ★★★ 2700 points` or
  `★ under 30 s · ★★ 14 s · ★★★ 12.5 s`). Park point challenges add the trick values, biggest first
  (`Backflip 400 · 360 300 · No-hander 200 · Tabletop 150`; the Plank: manual per second and hop) and "More tricks
  before you land = bigger ×. Manual after landing to keep it going!" (a manual keeps the combo from banking).
- **Countdown:** the challenge name and the same star line under 3-2-1 (Go on the map and Again skip the ring card).
- **Banner:** the next star still in reach: `Jumps 1/3 · 900 → ★★ 1500`, `★★★ by 14.6 s`.
- **Combo line** (game and lab): `Backflip + 360  700 ×2 = 1400`.
- **Result card:** `860 more points for ★★★` / `0.6 s faster for ★★★` (from this run's stars; a time gap rounds up to
  a tenth, never 0.0), and `🔓 Cone Zone is open!` when a new star opens locked challenges.
- Every number comes from `goal`, `TRICK_INFO` or `MOVE_INFO` (`src/game/targets.ts`), so retuning keeps it honest.
- Tests: 298 passing (`tests/targets.test.ts` new). `hub.mjs` 165, `game.mjs` 62.

## Nov 7: fixes from the user's first look at the loop

- **No more plain text between screens.** Every screen change is a page load, and the styles arrive with the game's
  code (slow over wifi from the dev server), so the raw page text used to show first. `play.html` now hides everything
  on the plain background until `main.ts` adds `styled` to the body (right after its CSS imports). A production build
  (`vite build`) would also make each page load much faster on the iPad; not done.
- **The result card has a way back to the list:** Again · 📍 Shredder Hills · Ride on. The 📍 button opens the area's
  list in the ride (the same one as the menu's 📍), with no page load.
- **Each empty star shows its price** in the list (map sheet, ride list, ring cards): `★ ☆45 ☆110 ⚙`. All three
  won: `★★★ replay +4 ⚙`. The old single "+20 ⚙" only showed the next star's pay, so 0 → 2 stars paid 65 while the
  list said 20. `starPays` in `progress.ts` replaces `nextPayFor`; a test holds it to `payFor` for every jump in stars.
- **A crash costs time, not the run** (the Nov 6 design's "crashes cost time, not a restart", brought in early,
  without gear). A crash in a challenge no longer fails it: you get up where you fell (2 s; a big crash adds seeing
  stars and winded) while the clock runs. On a Park challenge the crashed jump doesn't count and the combo in
  progress is lost (`Run.risk`, cleared when the combo banks: `Tick.banked`). Leaving the spot, falling off the plank
  and running out of time still fail. Gear and armor are still the later project.
- `checks/hub.mjs` now accepts the real map picture (`public/map/colorado.png` is in) and checks the star prices, the
  result card's 📍 button and the styled page.

## Nov 7: the ladder's weak spots

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-ladder-weak-spots-design.md`; plan:
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-07-ladder-weak-spots.md`. Every decision made while building is a `Ruling:` line in
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-07-ladder-weak-spots-ledger.md`. Robot numbers are in the ladder table below.

- **Table Row XL and Whoop-de-Doo are open to every bike.** The Pop 6 locks are gone. XL needs 5★ and Whoop-de-Doo 4★.
  The bare hardtail lands XL for 1★. Whoop-de-Doo's 2★ went from 2000 to 2500, because the bare hardtail reached 2250.
- **Cone Zone has 10 gates.** They're 7 m apart with a ±2 m swing (`SLALOM` in `src/lab/course.ts`; the robots and
  `game.mjs` read the gates from the course). The area around it got longer and wider. The robots now ride planned
  weaves with speed caps. **The spec's aim failed here:** bikes stay within about 0.3 s of each other on every layout
  tried (6 m ±2.5 m, 6 m ±2, 7 m ±2, 8 m ±2.5). The targets 30 / 14 / 12.5 are provisional.
- **The Plank has a gap to hop.** The narrow plank has a 1.8 m gap halfway (`PLANKS[0].gap`), with lip bars and a lip
  line. Rolling in is "Fell off the plank". The challenge counts only the narrow plank; the wide one is practice. The
  tip says "Manual the narrow plank, hop the gap, manual again". The robots hop the gap (`gapHop` styles).
- **Boulder Dash has a step-down and boulders** on its fast straight: a 0.45 m lip, then three boulders too big to plow
  (`CHUTE.stepU`, `CHUTE.rocks`). 3★ moved to 10.5 s. The tip says "Brake before the boulders!".
- **Hairpin Hollow climbs a hillside.** Each leg rises 2 m (`TRAIL.rise`), so the top is about 10.7 m up.
  - Legs 2 and 4 have step-downs facing down the trail (a 0.35 m kick and a 0.6 m drop). Ridden up, each is about a
    1.2 m climb.
  - The climb rules cover every leg.
  - A rock-lined dirt platform past the top lets you turn round (↕), and riding past the finish isn't a 10 m drop.
  - The hairpin pads have closed rings of rocks. The edge rocks on this trail are bigger and closer than elsewhere.
  - A new test rides 288 slow, steering runs into every pad and the platform and checks that no bike drops more than 1.5 m.
  - New targets: ↑ 120 / 78 / 63, ↓ 90 / 44 / 42, ↕ 240 / 130 / 112.
- **Final review** (one fresh reviewer) found falls of 4-11 m off the new hillside: gaps in the pad rings, plus an open
  side and a corner gap on the platform. Fixed with a test that failed first. It also caught that `game.mjs` put the
  bike inside the hill for the Hairpin ↑ check, and a 0.24 m kick at the foot of the step-down landings. Both fixed.
- **Deferred minors:**
  - The plank's far-half line assist ends 2.1 m early: `after` should be `x1 - gap[0]`.
  - Kid text: "Get on the plank" could say "narrow plank". "pop the step-downs" could be "pop the yellow lips".
  - A stale `boulderU` comment.
  - The Switchbacks start ring may clip the corner of the bottom hairpin pad (not checked visually).
- Tests: 282 passing. `hub.mjs` 159, `game.mjs` 58.

## Nov 6: progression loop (rider picker, map, garage, shop, cogs, stars, parts, locks)

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-progression-loop-design.md`. The plan's per-task notes and rulings are in
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-06-progression-loop/` (`progress.md` has every Ruling: line). This replaces the Nov 3 "one
page" menu: progress is back, built on the bike rules. The currency is **cogs ⚙** everywhere (never bolts).

**What a kid sees, in order** (`/play.html`, `src/game/main.ts` picks the screen from the URL):

1. **Who's riding?** (`hub/who.ts`) Two riders, names editable (✎), Ride!. No bike buttons any more.
2. **The map** (`hub/map.ts`): Colorado's Front Range full screen, with buttons drawn on top: the Garage (Lafayette),
   the Shop, and Shredder Hills. Tapping Shredder Hills opens its sheet: the 15 challenges in Trail / Downhill / Park
   groups with stars, what the next star pays, Go (or the lock in kid words), then "Just ride" warps.
3. **The ride** (`main.ts`): the park, glowing rings, countdown, result card with cogs earned. Swap button for owned
   bikes, a 🗺 Map button in the menu to leave. Leaving mid-run pays and saves nothing.
4. **Garage** (`hub/garage.ts`): your bike on its stand with the rider on it, a glowing dot per slot (Suspension, Tires,
   Brakes, Cockpit, Drivetrain). Tabs: Parts (fit or take off what you own), Paint (free colors), Specs (the sheet and
   the eight 0-10 bars). "Ride this one" picks the bike.
5. **Shop** (`hub/shop.ts`): bikes and parts you don't own, with the stand showing what you'd get and only the stats
   it moves. Buy is safe to double tap (it never charges twice or goes below 0).

Save: `shredder-save`, version 2: two riders, each with cogs, the bike they ride, owned bikes (parts, fitted, paint),
stars per challenge and how many times each has paid. A v1 or corrupt save loads as two fresh riders keeping their
names. Both riders start on the Hand-me-down Hardtail with 0 cogs. A saved `riding` bike that isn't owned, or an
unknown part id, falls back to the hardtail or the stock part.

**Stats** (`src/game/stats.ts`): eight 0-10 bars, each a fixed blend of Tune settings, so a part's cost always shows
(Trail: Climb, Speed; Downhill: Grip, Stop, Stable, Plow; Park: Pop, Flick). Pickup feeds Speed, skid feeds Grip, quick
steering and tight turns feed Flick. Stat locks are set clear of every reachable value (0.15) so a float wobble can't
flip one: Boulder Dash is 5.9 because 5.7 and 6.1 are reachable, the Plank 3.6 because 3.4 (bare) and 4.0 are.

**Adding a part** (`src/game/catalog.ts`, `PARTS`): one entry with `id`, `bike`, `slot`, `name`, `spec` (the real
numbers line), `price`, `ride` (deltas ADDED to the bike's base, only keys that slot may touch in `SLOT_KEYS`), optional
`spin`, `headAngle`, `weight`, and `does` (one kid sentence; say the cost too). No code anywhere asks which part or bike
it is. Every slot has exactly one `stock` part. Guards: `tests/catalog.test.ts` (one stock per slot, slot keys, geometry
and ride agree, price shape), `tests/stats.test.ts` (every key a part moves feeds a bar; every trade-off part shows a loss;
every upgrade moves a bar by 0.3), `tests/challenges.test.ts` (no stat lock within 0.15 of any reachable value, which
tries every part mix), `tests/pacing.test.ts` (prices against the pay). Then rerun the ladder (below).

**Adding a challenge** (`src/game/challenges.ts`, `CHALLENGES`): `id`, `name`, `group`, `spot` (a station key in
`src/lab/course.ts`; the kid name is `PLACE_NAMES` in `src/game/areas.ts`), `tell` (one sentence), `goal` (`gates`,
`jumps`, `lap`, `plank` or `run`; times are [limit, 2★, 3★], Park is points [1★, 2★, 3★]) and an optional `need`
(`stars`, `stat`, `bike`, `cleared`, `hint`). Then add its id to `AREAS[].challenges` in `areas.ts`. A timed run also needs
start/finish (and `via`) lines in `course.runs`, keyed by the challenge id; `runEvent` in `src/game/spots.ts` reads them.
Guards: `tests/challenges.test.ts` (unique ids, exactly the area's challenges, run ticks, locks), `tests/areas.test.ts`
(every spot has a name and is a station), `tests/pacing.test.ts`. Set the targets from robots:

    npx vite-node checks/ladder-robots.ts                      # everything: a table and a rule verdict per challenge
    ONLY=goatUp,coneZone npx vite-node checks/ladder-robots.ts # some challenges (GROUP=trail|downhill|park)
    LOAD=a,b  BOOST=4 BOOSTTIME=0.6  VERBOSE=1  npx vite-node checks/ladder-robots.ts

It rides each challenge on each loadout through the game's own challenge code, with the downhill boost on Downhill
runs. A full sweep is slow and starves the browser checks of CPU: don't run both at once (hub.mjs failed 7 lines that way).
The old robots (`climb-robots`, `downhill-robots`, `showcase-robots`, `jump-lanes`, `slalom`, `pump-laps`) take `BIKE=`
and `PARTS=stock|all|<part ids>` and use the new ids.

**`?tune=1`.** The Tune drawer and its warps are for the grown-up and the browser checks, not the boys. Without
`?tune=1` there is no Tune button and saved Tune values never touch a ride: every ride starts from the defaults plus the
bike's loadout. With it, `loadTune()` loads the saved values (bike numbers still win on a swap). Tune saves every number,
so press Reset in it after changing defaults. `tune.ts` itself did not change.

**Downhill boost.** A clean trick landing on a Downhill run gives `DOWNHILL_BOOST` (5 m/s for `trickBoostTime`) in
`challenges.ts`. At 3 it did nothing; 5 is the knee (about 0.6 s on Goat Ladder ↓; 6 adds only 0.07 s).

## Nov 5: bike rules (energy, speed wobble, trade-offs, four new places)

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-bike-rules-design.md`; plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-05-bike-rules.md`.
Every rule is a Tune setting (0 = off) and each bike sets its own numbers in `BIKE_RIDE` (`src/lab/bikes.ts`); no code
asks which bike it is. **On the iPad, press Reset in Tune**: Tune saves every number, so old saves hide the new ones.

- **Energy, everywhere** (`src/lab/energy.ts`, a ring round the stick knob). Pushing the stick up costs it; a steady
  pace (stick under about a third) costs little; coasting, cruising on the flat, the air and standing still refill it.
  Without a push your legs only give their steady share, so a steep pitch makes you slow, not tired; pushing up a
  pitch pays the hill in full. A full sprint on the flat empties the tank in about 10 s (`energyTank`).
  Out of energy on an incline: a foot goes down until the legs are back (half a tank). On the flat: soft legs, you
  keep rolling. Braking to a stop is resting by choice. "Pushing hard uses energy" is said once; "Burnt! Catch your
  breath" / "Burnt! Ease off" when it runs out.
- **Speed wobble, every bike** (`src/lab/wobble.ts`). Above the bike's `wobbleSpeed` steering gets touchier, rough
  ground and rock bonks knock the heading (by `wobbleKick`), and swinging the bars builds a hidden level that wipes you
  out ("Speed wobble!"). Braking drains it fast. You see it as rattle sound, a shaking camera and bike, and a red edge.
  Clean landings don't kick; a bobble does.
- **Trade-offs per bike** (numbers that ended up different from the spec in bold):

  | | Dirt Jumper | Bolinas | Enduro |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Energy cost | 1 | 0.8 (best climber) | **1.1** |
  | Legs | 3.8 | 4.4 | **4.2** (and pedal pickup **4.2**) |
  | One gear | tops out at 8.5 m/s, bogs on steep pitches (**gearBog 0.4**) | — | — |
  | Wobble speed / kick | 10 m/s / **1.4** | 14 / **1.0** | 18 / 0.3 |
  | Suspension | — | — | soaks 40% of a lazy pop, plows rocks under 0.35 m |
  | Trick spin | 1.35 | 0.9 | **0.46** (backflip 1.85 s) |

- **Tricks run on physics**: every bike can try every trick; a move lands once it's about 80% round, so whether it
  fits is the bike's spin against its air. "Not enough air for a Backflip! Try a bigger jump" (or "pop it").
  The dirt jumper fits two backflips in one air on the rhythm doubles; the enduro can't backflip until Jumps XL, and
  only with a perfect pop.
- **Four new places** (`src/lab/showcase.ts`, in Spots and Tune's warps): **Rhythm** (five doubles, the dirt
  jumper's, trick points), **Rock chute** (its own 20 m hill on the south strip, rough the whole way, the enduro's,
  a timed run), **Switchbacks** (east side, four hairpins and two climbing legs with gravel, the Bolinas's, a timed run),
  **Jumps XL** (beside Jumps L, the perfect-pop table). Timed runs use start and finish lines (`runEvent` in
  `src/game/spots.ts`).
- **Collision fixes the robots and the final review found**: a bike touching a rock while moving along it no longer
  gets pinned for good (it was frozen with speed on the dial), and slides along it only where the body still fits; a
  bike wedged with no way to slide stops, so turning in place frees it; the enduro plows only rocks it meets head on;
  a near-vertical rock face under the bike isn't ridden as ground (it used to launch the bike up at 30+ m/s).
  Tests in `tests/rockPin.test.ts` ride the real course into the trail edges.
- **Foot down looks right** (the user: the leg "detaches from the body"). The planted leg used to start its thigh out
  at the foot's side and stretch to a ground it couldn't reach. Now the knee is solved in 3D from the real hip
  (`kneeAt3` in `src/bike/rider.ts`), the foot goes just outside the hip, and the bike leans onto it by just enough
  for the leg to reach: about 17° on the dirt jumper, 22° on the Bolinas, 26° on the enduro.

Robot results (Nov 5, `checks/showcase-robots.ts` and the climb robots):

## Decisions so far, and why

- **Feel first.** "If riding doesn't feel good, the other mechanics won't make it fun." The bike feel is
  tuned in the lab, then brought into the game.
- **Stay in the browser** (three.js on iPad Safari). Revisit only if measured memory or frame rate fails.
- **"Ride the highlighted things"** replaced "ride everything":
  - Yellow lips and lip bars mark jumps.
  - Obvious ways up are shown.
  - Hints round the Jump button say what you can do right now.
- **Game loop in the park, not the neighborhood (yet).** The user worried an exact copy of the neighborhood
  limits tricks.
- **New direction (brainstorm, no design doc yet):**
  - The neighborhood is home base: garage, trick practice, maybe quests.
  - The family car is a portal to real trails: Doudy Draw, Heil Valley Ranch, West Magnolia and more.
  - Trails are cut down to the interesting blue-and-harder stretches, with no long flat miles.
  - Black diamonds need the right bike and skills.
  - About 3 big wipeouts send you home with a broken bike, fixed with parts from easier trails or quests.
  - There's always a default bike with bottom stats, so a bust never stops play.
- **Climbs and downhills are worth building.** The climb + downhill try-out is what the boys love most so far.
  The user: "I think we have pretty good mechanics. Let's start looking at other parts."

### Shared ride engine (`src/lab/`)

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `ride.ts` | `createRide(canvas, opts)`, the engine both pages use. Holds course, physics, look, rider rig, camera, HUD, controls, sound and fx; hooks for intent, step, gate and frame. Handles climb callouts, ledge/log hints and the gravel stick zone. |
| `bike.ts` | Kinematic lab bike: steering, speed, pumping, jumps, tricks, crashes, and the climb rules (`climbStops`, `footDown`). |
| `course.ts` | The lab park, built in code: lanes of tabletops, the hill line, pump track, planks, walls, the climb and descent. Exposes `lines` (jump lips), `stations` (warps), `climb` (areas, gravel, ledges and logs, start and top lines). |
| `touch.ts` | On-screen controls: left stick (jumps to the thumb), JUMP (tap to hop, hold to pop, swipe for tricks), BRAKE. Hint modes: air, ledge, log, downhill, ground. |
| `tune.ts` | Every number, with the Tune panel's slider groups. |
| `camera.ts` | Chase camera. Pulls in for `wall`/`rail`; rises over everything else, including `rock`. |

- **Conventions:**
  - yaw 0 faces +x; forward is (cos yaw, 0, −sin yaw).
  - The center ground probe snaps up steps up to 0.45 m (STEP_UP).
  - The body capsule's bottom is 0.35 m above the ground (BODY.lift, `src/world/physics.ts`). Anything taller
    than that blocks the body, and anything under 0.45 is rolled over.
- **Surfaces** (`src/world/physics.ts`): ground, grass, dirt, ramp, wall, rail, rock.
  - `rock` is for trail boulders and logs. The camera sees over it.
  - On a climb, hitting a rock only bonks you. Elsewhere, hitting one faster than `rockCrash` (5 m/s) is a
    wipeout.
- **Profile helpers** in `course.ts` build a trail's height along its run:
  - `flat`, `slopeTo(deg, absoluteHeight)`, `concave`, `convex`, `wave`.
  - `kick(absoluteHeight, lipDeg)` and `landing(deg, R)`, which lands to height 0.
  - `extrude` turns a profile into a solid: hillside, dirt overlay.
  - `heightAt(profile, u)` gives the height anywhere along it.
- **Jump lines** carry a `yaw`, so lips work in any direction. Lips feed the pop timing and the landing assist.
- **Rider squash fix** (`src/bike/rider.ts`): the landing squash spring is now stepped in 1/120 s slices and
  clamped. A slow frame after a hard landing used to blow the rider up into a giant dark shape.

### The climb + downhill try-out (in the lab park, so in both pages)

This started as a throwaway spike and the boys now love it. Stations: **Climb** (the bottom) and **Summit**
(top of the downhill, facing down). It sits east of the park centre at about x 52–116, z 62–95.

- **Climb rules** (only inside the climb areas):
  - **Legs, not the stick, set speed uphill.** Push is limited to `legs × effort / speed`: full effort when the
    stick is over 0.3 up, `steadyLegs` (0.7) otherwise. Steep pitches slow you unless you carry speed in.
  - **Foot down ("dab")** when you slow under 1 m/s on an uphill, with a short pause. Each one counts.
    Callouts:
    - "carry more speed into the steep bits" for a stall.
    - "gravel needs a little push on the stick" on gravel.
    - "steer round the rocks" just after hitting a rock.
  - **Ledges:**
    - The front wheel meeting a step over 0.15 m is "Bonk! Foot down / tap Jump to hop up ledges".
    - "tap to hop the ledge!" shows by the Jump button as you get close.
    - A hop from a stop pushes you forward (`hopPush`). Anywhere under 1 m/s it works, so you can get over the
      log too.
  - **Gravel:**
    - It adds drag (`looseDrag`).
    - It needs at least `looseMin` (0.15) stick.
    - Over `looseLimit` (0.6), the back wheel spins with dust and "Easy!". You crawl on `spinPower` (0.6) of your
      leg power, but never stall or put a foot down for it (Nov 5; it used to stop you dead for good).
    - A green band on the stick shows the right range, and "faster" reads "a little push".
  - **Timer:** crossing the yellow start line starts it. At the top: "Cleaned it!" (no feet down) or "Made it!
    N feet down", with the time.
- **Climb layout** (three legs, about 11 m of climbing):
  1. Leg 1 (east, z 70): a run-up, a pitch, a rock garden (rocks across the middle, so you weave), a ledge,
     a gravel pitch.
  2. Switchback 1: a narrow flat hairpin round a boulder, with rocks on the outside.
  3. Leg 2 (west, z 77): a pitch, two steps. Then a pitch split by a line of rocks: gravel all the way on
     one side, or a slab ledge then dirt on the other.
  4. Switchback 2.
  5. Leg 3 (east, z 84): a gravel pitch, a narrow gap between two big rocks plus one more to dodge, a
     ledge, the steep 19° punch, the top line.
- **Summit hairpin, then the downhill** (west, z 91; robots top out at 24 mph cruising, 29 mph with the stick up):
  1. A roll-in.
  2. Step-down table 1. Overshooting lands on more downhill, which is safe.
  3. A rock-garden pitch with a soft crest, so you can't fly over. Each row leaves one lane: left, right, left.
  4. A full-width log ("tap to hop the log!", shown further out at speed).
  5. A 0.7 m drop onto a slope.
  6. Step-down table 2.
  7. Down to the park, pointing at the pump track.

  Hitting a rock or the log at speed is a wipeout: "brake for the rocks · tap Jump for logs".
- **Trail edges:** a continuous row of rocks lines both edges of every leg, as one merged mesh. A cross row
  closes the low grass beside the run-up. There's no way round the obstacles on the grass.
- **Robot results** (`checks/climb-robots.ts`, `checks/downhill-robots.ts`):

  | Rider | Result |
  |---|---|
  | Smart (full stick on dirt, 0.35 on gravel, hops) | Cleans the climb in ~35–37 s, either side of the split |
  | Lets go on gravel | 8 feet down |
  | Cruises the whole climb | 17 feet down, ~71 s |
  | Stick fully up on the climb | Stuck spinning on the first gravel |
  | Downhill, braking to ~7 m/s and hopping | Clean |
  | Downhill, letting it run | Wipes out in the rock garden |
  | Downhill, never hopping | Stopped at the log |

- **Tune groups:** "Climbs (try-out)" for legs, steady share, foot-down speed and pause, the three gravel
  numbers, roll-up height and hop push. `rockCrash` sits with the wall settings.
